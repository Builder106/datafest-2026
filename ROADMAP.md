# DataFest-2026 Roadmap

Data analysis pipeline, exploratory models, and presentation roadmap.

## v1.1 — Feature Engineering & Cleaning

- **Automated Pipeline**: Robust missing data imputation and outlier detection.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis**: Statistical correlation and dimensional reduction (PCA, UMAP).

## v1.2 — Modeling & Insight Delivery

- **Predictive Model Evaluation**: Cross-validated ensemble models and feature importance metrics.
- **Executive Presentation Deck**: Clean, accessible visualizations tailored for presentation delivery.

## Out of Scope

- Overfitted black-box models lacking interpretability
- Unreproducible manual spreadsheet manipulations
